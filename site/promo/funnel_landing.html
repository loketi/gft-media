🎯 Funnel & Landing Page Playbook

1. Why Landing Pages?

Most businesses boost posts and send traffic directly to WhatsApp or Facebook pages. The problem is low conversion: people don’t get enough info, leads are unqualified, and results are weak. A landing page acts as a filter and trust-builder: showing who you are, what you offer, why choose you, and a clear CTA to WhatsApp.

2. Landing Page Flow

Ad → Landing Page → WhatsApp Chat

  • Headline with clear benefit
  • Short intro text
  • Key services (bullets/icons)
  • Testimonial or trust element
  • WhatsApp button (floating + in body)

3. Domain Options

Phase 1: Generic subdomain (e.g., bridal-shop.surge.sh) ✅ Free & instant ❌ Less professional

Phase 2: Custom domain (~KES 1,500–2,000/year). ✅ Builds trust, reusable, professional.
